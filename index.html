Athletic mobility system

Three components woven into every week: movement prep before lifting, lateral + plyo work on Tue and Fri, and a cooldown after Saturday. Strength and mobility progress together.

Movement prep — lower body days (Mon, Sat)
Daily protocol · 6–8 min
Hip, ankle + lateral activation
⌄
  • 1
    Lateral band walk
    15 steps each direction · Band above knees · Stay low · Wakes glute medius for lateral stability
  • 2
    Hip 90/90 rotations
    8/side · On floor, both knees at 90° · Rotate hips side to side · Unlocks hip internal + external rotation
  • 3
    Ankle circles + dorsiflexion
    10 circles each direction → knee-to-wall stretch 30s/side · Ankle mobility is the #1 limiting factor for squat depth
  • 4
    Glute bridge with abduction
    10 reps · Band above knees · Bridge up, push knees out at top · Activates glutes in both sagittal and lateral plane
  • 5
    World's greatest stretch
    4/side · Lunge → elbow to floor → rotate and reach · Hits hips, thoracic spine, and ankle in one move

Daily protocol · 6–8 min
Thoracic + shoulder prep
⌄
  • 1
    Thoracic rotation (quadruped)
    8/side · Hand behind head, rotate elbow to ceiling · Opens thoracic spine for pressing and rowing
  • 2
    Shoulder CARs
    5 slow circles each direction · Full range, no compensation · Lubricates shoulder joint before load
  • 3

    15 reps · Arms straight, pull band to chest width · Activates rear delts and mid-traps

Plyo progression · Weeks 1–24
3 phases · Progress safely
Plyo phases
Phase 1Phase 2Phase 3
⌄
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–6 · Landing mechanics
  • 1
    Focus: stick every landing
    Knees track over toes, hips absorb impact. Quality over height or distance. If you can't stick the landing, the load is too high.
  • 2
    Drills: box jump, broad jump, lateral bound
    Low box. Short distances. Full recovery between reps (5–8s). Learn the patterns before loading them.
Phase 2 · Weeks 7–14 · Load and distance
  • 1
    Add height, distance, and reps
    Increase box height. Jump farther. Add a rep to lateral bounds. Still full recovery between reps.
  • 2
    Introduce reactive drills
    Lateral shuffle + sprint. Cone hops. Reduce rest slightly (3–4s). Begin training continuous lateral movement.
Phase 3 · Weeks 15+ · Speed and reactivity
  • 1
    Continuous hops, reactive direction change
    No stick between lateral hops. Reactive shuffles off a visual or auditory cue. True athletic movement quality.
  • 2
    Add weighted plyo
    Light dumbbell goblet jump squat, weighted lateral bound. Only add load once bodyweight form is clean at speed.

Key reminders
Don't eat too little. Eating significantly below 1,750 kcal tells your body to slow metabolism and hold fat — the opposite of your goal.
Fuel plyo and lift days. Eat complex carbs (oats, rice, sweet potato) before Friday and Monday. Plyo is carb-dependent.
Sleep is nutrition. Less than 7 hours raises hunger hormones and blunts muscle building. Treat it like a macro.

How to track your recomp
The scale is not your primary metric. You could stay at 128 lbs for 6 weeks while losing fat and gaining muscle simultaneously. Use these four methods together.

Tracking methods

Use all four together for an accurate picture.

1
InBody rescan
Every 8 weeks. Your most important metric — shows actual fat loss and muscle gain regardless of scale weight. Schedule your next scan now.
2
Weekly scale
Days 2–5 of your cycle only. Same time, same conditions. 4-week rolling average — single readings mean nothing.
3
Monthly measurements
Waist, hips, chest, each thigh. Often change weeks before the scale moves.
4
Strength + mobility log
Record every lift weight. Also note plyo milestones — first clean lateral bound stick, first broad jump PR. Athletic progress is part of your goal.
